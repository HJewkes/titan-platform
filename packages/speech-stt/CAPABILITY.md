# speech-stt: use this when

<!-- One to three sentences for a reader deciding whether to reuse this or build something new. Feeds CAPABILITIES.md. -->

You compare a script with what a recogniser heard, or port Python that calls `difflib`: a CPython-parity `SequenceMatcher`, `align` to time script words from a transcript, `diff` and `normalize` to score a take, the `Word` and `Transcript` schemas, and a pure TS `resample` (24 kHz to 16 kHz without ffmpeg). Synthesis is `speech-tts`; the recogniser backend is not here yet.
