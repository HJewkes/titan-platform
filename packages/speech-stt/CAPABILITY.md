# speech-stt: use this when

<!-- One to three sentences for a reader deciding whether to reuse this or build something new. Feeds CAPABILITIES.md. -->

You compare a script with what a recogniser heard, or port Python that calls `difflib`: a CPython-parity `SequenceMatcher` and `roundHalfEven` (Python's `round`), `align` to time script words from a transcript, `diff` and `normalize` to score a take, the `Word` and `Transcript` schemas, and a pure TS `resample` (24 kHz to 16 kHz without ffmpeg). `whisperStt` transcribes audio to word-timed `Transcript`s through transformers.js Whisper (optional peer), cached by audio hash, and declares `capabilities.tokenLogprobs: false` (no word probabilities or segment confidence). `wav2vec2Recognizer` gives espeak-style IPA phones per 20 ms frame (fp32 by default, at parity with the PyTorch model; q8 opt-in changes verdicts) and exposes the CTC logits for forced alignment. `alignWords` force-aligns a script to those logits for word starts within a frame or two, with a per-voice-family offset. Synthesis is `speech-tts`.
