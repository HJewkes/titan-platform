# speech-vet: use this when

<!-- One to three sentences for a reader deciding whether to reuse this or build something new. Feeds CAPABILITIES.md. -->

You hold narration text and must say how its hard words are read before any audio exists: `[word](/phonemes/)` overrides and `[word](=reading)` declarations, a heteronym check of G2P tokens against those declarations, a `lexicon.v1` file of rewrites, respellings and phonemes, dotted-letter initialisms, and `normalize()`, composable steps that spell out numbers, decimals, currency, ordinals, clock times and per-unit rates. Once audio exists, `chunkWordVerdicts`, `detectHallucinations` and `detectTailArtifact` judge a recognizer's phones against espeak-style expected phones (a port of the audiobook phone check, at verdict parity). `espeakCliG2p` supplies those expected phones by running the espeak-ng binary you install (GPL, called as a process, not bundled). It loads no model; tokens and phones come from the caller. Synthesis belongs to speech-tts.
