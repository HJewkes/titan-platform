Device gates (`device-*` step ids, such as `device-confirm`) answer only to the owner at the factory's terminal: the store's `authorize` refuses every class the authority table's HW-CO row does not name, and any channel but `factory-cli`, so a remote owner, a signed resolve-proof, a coordinator, the decider, an agent and automation all leave the gate pending with a named reason. Other factory gates are unchanged and still admit no rule delegate.
