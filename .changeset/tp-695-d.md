The owner digest has a "Decided for you" section after Needs you. It lists the questions the decider answered in the window, read from `agent-chat digest --json` (`ledger.decided`). Each line shows the answer, the class, and, when agent-chat sends them, the confidence, the principle ids and how to reverse the decision. A decision awaiting audit also shows its overrule command. The section ends with the 7-day reversal rate per class. Decisions only use the words left under the 400-word limit, and the "and N more" line counts the rest. With an older agent-chat the section is left out.
