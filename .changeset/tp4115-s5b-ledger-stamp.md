The script stage now stamps `new_terms` on the first beat that speaks each spine-ledger handle, when the ledger defines the item in that beat's chapter and the model left it out. Each stamp is reported as a non-gating `ledger-stamp` check. The Q-E17 repair hint now names each ledger-unlisted item (a pin, reveal or card), its beat and the ledger handles that chapter may use, and names an item shown before the chapter that defines it.
