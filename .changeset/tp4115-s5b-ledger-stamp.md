The script stage now stamps `new_terms` on the first beat that speaks each spine-ledger handle, when the ledger defines the item in that beat's chapter, the model left it out, the glossary holds the handle and the beat and chapter are under their new-term limits, so a stamp adds no error row. Each stamp is reported as a non-gating `ledger-stamp` check. The Q-E17 repair hint now names each ledger-unlisted item (a pin, reveal or card), its beat and the ledger handles that chapter may use, and names an item shown before the chapter that defines it.
