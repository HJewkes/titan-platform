Shepherd accepts a g10-adversary release at the reviewed head when the PR's current head is Shepherd's own clean merge-up of it (TP-2826). The run's update-branch record must show Shepherd made the head, and a tree probe must prove it is a clean merge of the base into the reviewed head. The release event keeps the reviewed head, and the probe's proof is recorded as a vouch to the current head. A conflicted merge, an extra commit, a merge Shepherd did not make or a hold of another class still needs a release at the current head.
