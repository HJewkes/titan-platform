Price a long-prompt tier. `PriceInput` takes an optional `longPrompt` (`abovePromptTokens` plus the five rates), which `syncPrices` and `reconcilePrices` store in six new nullable `price` columns; `reconcilePrices` counts a changed tier as an update. Migration 13 (`PRICE_LONG_PROMPT_MIGRATION_NAME`) adds the columns and recreates `request_cost` so a request whose `context_tokens` is strictly above the threshold prices every component, output included, at the long rates. A row with no tier prices exactly as before.
