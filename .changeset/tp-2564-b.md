Shepherd: a run held `g10-adversary: changed files unread at registration` reads its changed files again at each CI read through a recorded `sh-g10-reclass` step (TP-2564 b). A whole read with no adversary touch lifts the hold as a re-class, keeping any parked hold; an adversary touch or a failed or truncated read keeps it.
