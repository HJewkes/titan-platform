`aggregate` fails closed on fix-proof input it cannot read. A `fix-proof/v1` line is parsed with fix-proof's `parseResultLine`, and it makes the tests member blocking when its verdict is `vacuous` or `no-tests`, when it names another head, or when it does not parse. Any other unrecognised non-empty `fixProof` also makes the tests member blocking.
