Shepherd gains `sh-no-reviewer-hold` and `sh-no-reviewer-wait` steps, not yet wired into the review phase. When the reviewer dispatch is refused, the hold step holds the run as `no-reviewer: <refusal> at <head>; TP-2446` and alerts the repo's seat once. If another hold is already in place, it leaves that hold alone and sends no alert. The wait resumes on release, head move, close or a 60-minute expiry. `noReviewerHeld` answers none with cause `not-started`, so a refused review is retried without counting a failed round.
