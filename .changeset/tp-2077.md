The adoption registry holds unshipped `baseline` entries beside shipped ones and is seeded with the 15 ranked rows of the 2026-10-08 bash-to-tool mining. Q10 `tool-adoption` counts a baseline's old pipelines from the window start, never flags it, and marks it `baseline` in the text table. Rows gain `row`, `status` and `note`; `task`, `pr`, `shipped` and `daysSinceShip` are null for a baseline. With nothing shipped and no `--since`, the window starts four weeks back. A new form on the old head with no flags is no longer counted.
