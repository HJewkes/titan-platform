Add the engine contract real ports read. `TargetSchema` reads `repo`, `caseSets`, `runnerRoot`, `unitTests`, `testProof` and `rungs`, and `renderCommand` fills their templates. Eval keys are unique per run, ladder and repeat, and σ pairs are matched by position. Each ladder holds a recorded lane slot and gets an exploit or explore fork from the fork schedule. The `ar-stop` outcome records `against` (with `case` and an explicit `locked`), the implementer (`mode`, `account`), lane, fork, L0 rows, micro counts and usage. The ratchet input carries the move record, the micro input the best's `baselineDir`, the plan input a spawn or resume `mode`. New dispatch steps are `ar-load-wait` and `ar-archive`, with optional `loadWait` and `archive` ports. Dead-end and bug rows name their idea and round. **Breaking for port implementers:** `ImplementedSchema` requires `agent` and `mode`.
