`shepherd register` returns an advisory `sizeFlag`, and the text output prints a warning line, when a PR registered with kind `security` or touching a guard area is over the 400 non-generated line slice cap (review-panel `securitySliceFlag`). The flag never holds a PR. A failed read, or one that runs past 15 s, gives no flag and never fails the registration.
