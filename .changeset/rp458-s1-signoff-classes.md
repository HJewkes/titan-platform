Shepherd's registration hold follows review policy 458's sign-off classes, given by the new pure `requiredSignoffs`. One correctness sign-off is the default. Size, a migration, a security kind with no sensitive path, and unread line counts no longer place a `g10-review` hold; they still pick the g10 (Opus) correctness reviewer. A store-migration touch now reaches that reviewer through its own `migrationTouch` reviewer fact, with Shepherd's migration globs (`store-migrations.ts`, `*-store.ts`, `*-migration*.ts` and Shepherd's store, events and train files), rather than through the hold it no longer gets. Only an adversarial path (authority, policy or security) places `g10-adversary`. That list now also covers the console's owner-tap and inbox actions, gate resolution, owner release (matched by name stem, so a helper split out of those files stays covered) and agent-chat's agent profiles. A hold placed by hand is unchanged, and so is the fail-closed `g10-adversary` hold for an unread file list.
