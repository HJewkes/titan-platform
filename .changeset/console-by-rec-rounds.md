Answer rounds the owner left silent past the by-rec wait (24 h by default) with their recommendations (TP-6773). The wait runs from when the sweep first saw the round, not from a `round.json` mtime the asker can backdate. The daemon sweeps hourly through `rounds.submit`'s pipeline, which writes owner-queue's `auto-by-rec` mark into the feedback; the decision log and the owner queue read the answerer from that mark, so the decisions bullet and the queue credit the `automation` resolver. The decider's ledger (`answers.jsonl`) now records only owner answers, so a sweep answer is never read back as the owner's precedent. The asker's notice for a sweep answer says the auto-by-rec sweep answered after the policy's wait and that the owner has not seen it; an owner answer keeps its notice. The gate relay and the plan-approval record refuse a sweep answer as "answered by automation, not the owner". `server/feedback-readers.test.ts` lists every feedback.json reader with how it treats the answerer, and fails on one it does not list. Each question is judged on `byRecRoundSubject`, so a cost named in the section's deciding text or context or in option details floors it as money. It skips after-answer rounds, Ship and plan-approval questions, and any question without a recommendation. An `auto-by-rec.json` marker, written once, stops repeats. It runs in shadow by default, writing only would-resolve rows to `auto-by-rec.jsonl`, until `auto-by-rec-policy.json` in the console state dir sets `mode: live`.
