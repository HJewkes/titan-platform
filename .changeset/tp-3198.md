Shepherd: with `shepherd.gateReset.heldRepair: "on"`, a run held only under `g10-review` or `g10-adversary` wakes its fixer on ci-red, conflict, FIX_FIRST and NO_REPRO. The hold stays, so each new head is reviewed again and nothing merges while it is held (TP-3198). The flag is absent by default, which keeps today's wait. A MERGE at a green head now releases its g10-review hold before a conflict leaves the round. A seat notice that times out is sent again after a backoff.
