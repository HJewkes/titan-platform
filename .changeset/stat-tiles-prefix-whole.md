A stat tile keeps a flush currency sign or version `v` with its number: "$1261" and "$13.41" draw as a whole, "v1" stays "v1" from its first frame, and a figure keeps all its decimals. A row of three or four tiles now shrinks to stay inside the template's side margins instead of running to the frame edge. The resolve, render and qa stage versions are bumped.
