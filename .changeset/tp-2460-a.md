`shepherd status` shows a run the workflow runtime parked in `recovery_required` after it recorded `sh-landed` or `sh-stopped` in phase `done` with its outcome. The stall reason stays in `stalled.reason` and the text `[stalled: ...]` note, and `phaseSince`, `totalMinutes` and `stage` stop at the outcome step, so a merged run no longer reads as "in review" for days. A recovery run with no outcome, and a paused run watching main CI after its merge, keep their live phase.
