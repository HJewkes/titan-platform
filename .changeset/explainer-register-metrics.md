`eval l0` reports the spoken register of a script against human explainer narration, as Q-V11 warning rows: number mentions (a run of number words is one mention), "you", "we", sentence length mean and CV, contractions, triads, colon reveals, "Y, not X" tails, semicolons and "let's". Each row warns when a script of 150 or more spoken words falls outside its band (`REGISTER_BANDS`); a shorter script reports values without warning. `registerCheck`, `spokenText` and `REGISTER_MEASURES` are exported.
