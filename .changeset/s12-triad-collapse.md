Accept a beat rewrite that collapses a list of three into labels: list items are carried by content terms, the list's count (or "N more") may be said, a pronoun "one" is no number, short beats get a 6-word length floor, and a collapse that keeps no item and nothing its sentence said is rejected (`list-emptied`). The triad directive and the rewrite guide now ask for every item in `labels_added`.
