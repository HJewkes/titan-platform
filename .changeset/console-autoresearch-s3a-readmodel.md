Read the C-256 S3a autoresearch fields on `#/autoresearch`. The header's σ names the case it was measured on and follows the engine's own lock and band width. The idea drawer shows the implementer's spawn or resume mode and account, the lane and fork, the noise it was judged against, each L0 check and the implement, eval and micro spend. Dead-end and bug rows link to their idea and show the round (TP-3867). Every new field is optional, so a run recorded before S3a still renders.
