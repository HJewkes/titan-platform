The reviewer brief now tells reviewers to leave out non-blocking notes that are cosmetic, on behaviour already on the base, or on gaps the PR description states, unless one would mislead a reader. It never covers a blocking item: a stated gap that leaves the done-when unmet, or a pre-existing defect the PR now relies on, still blocks. Cosmetic notes are never filed as follow-ups; a non-cosmetic stated gap may be. A convergence review still lists Nit and Pre-existing items in its own section.
