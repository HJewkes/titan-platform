`shepherd.review.panel: on` now reviews a head with its planned panel. The correctness member keeps `sh-review:<head>` and `sh-await-verdict:<head>`; every other member runs `sh-review:<head>:<shape>` and `sh-await-verdict:<head>:<shape>` at its planned profile with its shape's brief, and `sh-review-panel:<head>` records the aggregate. A panel MERGE carries `panel: {satisfiesG10, degraded, members}`, and a panel FIX_FIRST carries each member's findings and the panel's defect class. A run whose intent recorded no plan continues as a single reviewer. `ReviewerFacts` gains `profile`, which a reviewer dispatch spawns at in place of the class's profile.
