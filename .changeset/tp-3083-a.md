Shepherd: a route-table approve-merge gate the head moves past, failed-rounds included, carries its escalation to the new head, so the owner is asked there again and the cancel never stands as an answer. New hold class `owner-review` records a review the owner assigned to a person; no Shepherd MERGE releases it. A `g10-review` hold with no hold event on record, or an unreadable history, no longer releases itself.
