`service check` prints within a 20 s budget (`SERVICE_CHECK_BUDGET_MS`) across all its `/health` probes, and reports the new cause `unresponsive` when the launchd or systemd pid is alive but every probe timed out, with each probe's latency in `detail.probeLatenciesMs`. A dead pid, a foreign pid on the port or no process stays `stale pid`.
