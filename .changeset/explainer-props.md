`explainer props --mp4-sha <sha> [--json]`: the props panel's read model. It finds the newest run whose export linked that mp4 (or answers `found: false`) and gives each template beat resolve kept its spec, slot ref and slot JSON Schema, the filled value with owner overrides, the overridden fields, the ids its cues lock, its start, end and still time from the words artifact, and a `saveBack` flag that stays off until the render reads the template fill (TP-2992 S3).
