`eval cold` scores reader gaps from the spoken words only (TP-3807). Both cold prompts now show each beat's spoken words and end, never the on-screen labels, since the reader-gap labels come from readers who had the narration only. Spoken terms are asked "what does X mean here?" in a call of their own per chapter, under the new rubric `l1-cold-spoken.md`, which does not count a sentence that only uses the word as a definition; that call has its own canary. A term inside a longer new label ("I2 verdict at head") is now asked on its own. `ColdChapter.canary` becomes `canaries`, one a call. `playbackTranscript` is renamed `spokenTranscript`. The new `readerGapScores` scores each labelled reader-gap group at its items' first questions, and `eval cold --labels <file>` prints it per run. Viewer-agreed terms are asked as `viewer-term` questions, and their zeros go to new `undefined_terms` and `undefined_terms_per_minute` columns, not `undefined_items`, so the chapter bars stay comparable with earlier runs; `cold-chapters.tsv` gains those columns, so write to a fresh bench. The eval_version changes.
