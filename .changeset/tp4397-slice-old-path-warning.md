The planning lint warns when a slice grows agent-chat `src` old path (TP-4397 F2). A slice with `repo` agent-chat whose owns fall under a sunset-manifest area the fold deletes gets one warning naming the fold task, unless it sets the new optional `carveOut` field. Surviving areas such as `src/broker` stay quiet. The warning never fails the lint, and the old paths are read from the sunset manifest.
