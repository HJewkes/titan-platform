M3-pre (`explainRoutes`) now reads an item's gloss from the clause its own cue word is spoken in (items that share a cue fall back to the clause naming them), and counts a spoken number as a gloss word unless it restates a digit on the label. On the v4 script it routes script-method's ten beats. `PaceItem` gains an optional `cue`. The L1 item judge no longer asks "explained?" of a PROMISE beat's items, and asks a CAVEAT beat's items why the picture holds without them; the new question changes the `L1-items` eval_version.
