Shepherd keeps its own reviewer's MERGE when a seat reviewer's WAIT, or a seat read that can clear, outlasts the seat-wait window. The next round at that head runs only the seat check again (`sh-seat-recheck`): it starts no reviewer and counts no failed round. Held rounds have a limit of their own: after six at one head, the owner's failed-rounds gate opens, naming the seat and its standing reason. A clear seat lets the MERGE go to the merge decision, a seat FIX_FIRST sends the head back, and a WAIT that still stands holds the MERGE again. Nothing merges until the seat check clears. A seat roster or transcript read that failed for a known-transient reason (a broker timeout, an unreachable or busy broker, or HTTP 5xx) is retried inside the seat-wait window instead of dropping the MERGE at once. A permission, missing-file or parse failure is not retried and blocks the head with no hold. A damaged transcript still blocks with no hold. While a seat holds the MERGE, `shepherd view` shows the hold as the PR's review wait.
