Shepherd's seat check no longer turns its own reviewer's MERGE into `none` on the first seat roster or transcript read that a busy broker timed out. A read that can clear is retried inside the seat-wait window, the same way a seat WAIT is, and fails closed when the window ends. A damaged transcript still blocks at once. While a seat WAIT or a retry holds the MERGE, `shepherd view` shows the hold as the PR's review wait.
