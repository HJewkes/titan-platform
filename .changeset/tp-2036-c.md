Add `remoteFactory(base, fetch)`, an HTTP client for the live factory named by the `remoteFactory` config: it reads `factory.gates-review` and the owner key ids in `/health`, and posts owner proofs to the two proof routes. Every request carries the client header, a 20 s timeout and never follows a redirect. The base must be a bare origin and paths plain absolute paths. Non-2xx reads, oversized or duplicate gate lists, control characters, and proof answers without outcomes are failures; a refused or timed-out connection is an `UnavailableError` naming the URL (TP-2036 c). Not wired yet.
