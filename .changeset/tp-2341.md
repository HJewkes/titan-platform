serve shuts down in bounded time: on SIGTERM it halts every sweep, releases its runs' leases before waiting on anything, gives running sweep ticks at most `SHUTDOWN_SWEEP_GRACE_MS` (5 s) together, then closes the daemon and the database. The `closed` log line carries the close time and any abandoned sweep, and the resync-at-start line carries `phaseMs`.
