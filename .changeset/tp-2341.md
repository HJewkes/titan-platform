serve shuts down in bounded time. On SIGTERM it halts every sweep, then releases its runs' leases before waiting on anything. Sweep ticks that are still running get at most `SHUTDOWN_SWEEP_GRACE_MS` (5 s) together. A tick still running at the end of the grace has its signal aborted, which kills the gate notifier's agent-chat child. Serve then closes the daemon and the database, and the serve verb exits the process. The `closed` log line carries the close time and any abandoned sweep, and the resync-at-start line carries `phaseMs`.
