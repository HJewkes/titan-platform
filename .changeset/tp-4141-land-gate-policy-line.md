An approve-merge gate whose prompt carries the reviewer's owner brief or a skill-diff line below its policy line now reads as a land gate, so a coordinator's mechanical merge, merge-at-head, gate batches and console taps act on it. Skill-path, ci-policy-weakened and ci-policy-malformed gates, and any merge gate showing a repo skill diff under another rule (an owner-gate seat or an escalation route), stay off the console tap, merge-at-head, standing answers, Ship answers and batches.
