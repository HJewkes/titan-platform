Add the citation contract for insight findings: `findingSchema` (a claim, at least one citation and a non-empty falsifier) and `verifyCitations`, plus `titan-miner insights verify-citations --findings <file>`. The command re-runs each cited question once per distinct question and args, checks each cited JSON pointer (numbers within a relative tolerance, 1 percent by default; anything else exactly), reports `ok`, `mismatch`, `missing-field`, `unknown-question` or `error` per citation, and exits non-zero on any that is not ok.
