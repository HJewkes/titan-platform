Factory gates become full owner cards: `decisionClassOf` maps each gate's policy row or step to its decision-routing D-row, and hitl owner items carry `class`, `irreversible`, `shown`, `onSilence` and a consequence per option, with `lint` naming any card rule a gate still breaks. `titan-factory needs` prints the class, door, on-silence line and consequences.
