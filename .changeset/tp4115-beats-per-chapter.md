The script stage plans one-idea beats. Its prompt asks each chapter for ceil(chapterS / 20) beats or more, each within Q-F8's 6-20 s, with continuation beats after the chapter's lead beat. A new `beat-length` gate, also part of the plan retry's faults, names each beat out of that band and the split its chapter needs. Q-F8's bounds come from one export (`BEAT_SECONDS`, `MAX_BEAT_NEW_NAMES`). A narrative structure may now declare `stakeFunctions`, the beat functions Q-W1 accepts as voicing the stake. `scqa` declares VERDICT and STAKE, because its bottom line comes first. Every other structure keeps STAKE only, and the named-role and number checks are unchanged.
