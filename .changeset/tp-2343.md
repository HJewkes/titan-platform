A `cancellation_unknown` execution terminal now carries the optional `usage` the harness reported before it was aborted, timed out or lost its cancellation acknowledgement, the same way a `failed` terminal does. `terminalFromHarnessResult` copies non-empty harness usage onto it and omits the key when nothing was spent.
