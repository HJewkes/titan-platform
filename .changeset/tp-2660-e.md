Shepherd no longer opens approve-merge when GitHub leaves `mergeable_state` unknown (TP-2660 e). Land re-reads with backoff for up to 20 minutes at the same head, then sends the repo's seat one `merge-unsettled` notice and keeps re-reading on each CI read. A new head starts its own wait. The owner gate opens only if the notice could not be sent.
