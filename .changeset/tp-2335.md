Pace Shepherd's self-deploys. The `sh-redeploy` step now queues the merge sha in `deploy-wanted.json` instead of spawning a deployer per merge, and `serve` runs a 60 s deploy pacer sweep that spawns the deployer through the same systemd-run path once merges have been quiet for 15 min, or 90 min after the first queued merge. Busy review and merge runs hold the spawn until that cap, and a rollback pause (`deploy-pause.json`) holds it until cleared. A deployer that records nothing is asked once more after 2 h, then the sha is dropped until the next merge. `/health` gains `deployPending`.
