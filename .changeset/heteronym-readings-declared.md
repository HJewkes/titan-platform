The script stage declares the heteronym readings a word's neighbours decide, so Q-V10 no longer stops a run on them. Every parsed draft or repair reply gets a `[w](=reading)` declaration where a closed-class rule decides: "minutes", and "minute" before a pause or a function word, is the time unit; "minute" after "a" or an intensifier is the adjective; a noun/verb heteronym after an article or possessive is the noun and after a modal, subject pronoun or infinitive "to" (never before an -s form) is the verb; "read" after a perfect or passive auxiliary is past and after a modal is present. An occurrence the rules leave open stays bare and still fails Q-V10. The l0 gate detail and the Q-V10 repair hint now name each undeclared word and its place in the line, numbers counted as words. A declared line now times, counts and captions as its plain words: the tts stage realigns the words a listener hears, so words.json and captions.vtt carry no `[w](=reading)` markup, and a cue may name a declared word; a beat opening on a declared word lands its uncued reveals on that word. The rewrite stage re-declares each parsed reply the same way, so a rewrite that drops a declaration its context decides is not reverted for Q-V10. Script stage version x16, rewrite x10, tts p6c.
