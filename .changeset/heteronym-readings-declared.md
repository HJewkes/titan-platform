The script stage declares the heteronym readings a word's neighbours decide, so Q-V10 no longer stops a run on them. Every parsed draft or repair reply gets a `[w](=reading)` declaration where a closed-class rule decides: "minutes", and "minute" before a pause or a function word, is the time unit; "minute" after "a" or an intensifier is the adjective; a noun/verb heteronym after a determiner is the noun and after a modal, subject pronoun or infinitive "to" is the verb; "read" after a perfect or passive auxiliary is past and after a modal is present. An occurrence the rules leave open stays bare and still fails Q-V10. The l0 gate detail and the Q-V10 repair hint now name each undeclared word and its place in the line. Script stage version x16.
