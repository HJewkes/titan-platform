Shepherd: a seat reviewer's failed transcript read no longer blocks every PR on the machine.
- A read that may clear, including the broker's "did not answer <frame>_result" refusal, blocks with a retry, so the seat wait holds Shepherd's MERGE.
- A read that fails the same every time clears with a warning only when the reviewer's records name another PR, or the roster places it in another agent's worktree.
- The read blocks when the reviewer's name or worktree is the PR's registered implementer's. With no evidence either way, it blocks with a retry and the reason says what was missing.
- The seat-check reason carries agent-dispatch's own message, with credentials, URLs and home paths scrubbed and the length capped (TP-2872).
