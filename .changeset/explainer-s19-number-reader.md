The rewrite guard's number reader reads "a" or "an" as one only before hundred, thousand, million or billion, so "a five-hour ceiling" is 5. Two numbers side by side read as a year only when both are ten or more, and a run that will not compose splits into its parts, so "one two" is 1 and 2. A unit tag after digits is dropped ("5 s" is 5, "5k" is 5000). The speller reads a dash between two numbers as "to", so "30–35%" is spoken "thirty to thirty-five percent".
