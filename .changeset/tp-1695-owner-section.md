`buildOwnerSection` builds the owner PR section (link, what it does, why it reaches you, pros, cons, before and after, next steps) plus a round@2 section and a pick-one question with a recommendation, from the PR facts, the pending gate, the run's owner-gate reason and the reviewer's verdict and owner brief. `whyAsked` maps every gate rule (seat owner-gate, merge-guard rows, authority MRG-AU unmet conditions, route escalations) to one plain sentence labelled deliberate, accidental or unclassified. A reviewed head behind the PR head is stated as a con.
