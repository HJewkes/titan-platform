Shepherd reviewer stopgap (TP-2804). A reviewer ask that times out is looked for during the session-start wait and, if it is still unseen, retried on the next round rather than held through the 1 h no-reviewer wait. A reviewer that exits with no verdict at the head gets one correction turn in its own session, which names the Verdict, PR and Head lines. A fresh reviewer follows only if that resume fails.
