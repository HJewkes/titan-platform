The reflection deposit is titled and keyed by the digest day the run gathered, not the day it ran, and a run from a later stage inherits that day from its base run. A run with no digest day files nothing and notes why. The exported text drops speech markup such as `[minutes](=time)`; citations stay. No stage version changes: reflection.md and the deposit are not stored artifacts.
