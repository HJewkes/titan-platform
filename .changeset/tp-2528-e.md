After Shepherd squash-merges a stacked parent, each direct `agent-chat/*` child is restacked with a merge-onto commit and a plain fast-forward push, retargeted to the default branch while its base is still the parent's branch, and recorded as `sh-restack:<pr>:<head>`; a conflict, guard miss or moved head pushes nothing and wakes the child's implementer (TP-2528 e).
