Opt-in per repo with `shepherd.repos.<repo>.stackRestack: true` (off by default): after Shepherd's own squash merge of a stacked parent, each direct `agent-chat/*` child is restacked with a merge-onto commit and a plain fast-forward push, then retargeted to the default branch while its base is still the parent's branch, and recorded as `sh-restack:<pr>:<head>`; a conflict, guard miss or moved head pushes nothing and wakes the child's implementer, and a failed child-list read records `skipped` so post-merge continues (TP-2528 e).
