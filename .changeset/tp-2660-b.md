Shepherd no longer withholds the merge-on-green grant for an unverified fix-proof (owner decision 303, reversing TP-2434). A fix-proof read that errors is read once more at the same head first. The unverified standing and its read reason are recorded on the merge evidence (`proofUnverified`) and in the evidence PR comment, and an unverified proof no longer asks the reviewer for an owner brief. The reviewer's unverified-proof brief no longer says a MERGE goes to the owner.
