Add `reviewPullRequest(request, ports, opts?)`, the one entry point over the panel's ports: it classifies, plans, spawns members in parallel under `memberName`, corrects a malformed reply once, retries a silent member once under a fresh name, and returns `aggregate`'s verdict. A repeat call with the same input reuses the roster rows. Also exports `memberName`, `SPAWN_REFUSED_REASON` and the `ReviewRequest`, `ReviewPorts`, `ReviewClock` and `ReviewOptions` types.
