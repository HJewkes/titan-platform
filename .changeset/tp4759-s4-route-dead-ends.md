Shepherd routes its three dead ends to the `sh-dead-end` seat gate (TP-4759 S4, owner decision 552) once `GATE_DEMOTIONS.deadEndSeat` is on; it stays off until S5. A fixer's exit with no push that told no seat, a fix-first-runaway that D8 leaves, and a failed-rounds escalation that D7 leaves now ask the registering seat. That includes a fix-proof exit and a repo with no single seat. With no resolvable seat, the owner's sent-back or approve-merge gate opens as before. A run recorded or paused on one of those older owner gates keeps it on replay.
