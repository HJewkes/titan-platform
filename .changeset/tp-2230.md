Splice the literal output of a substitution into the word that `eval` or a shell's `-c` runs, where the substitution sits. A push or merge reached through `eval "$(cat <<'E' … E)"`, a cat here-string, `echo`, `printf` or a backquote, including the arithmetic form `eval "$(cat <<'E' $((X)) E)"` and output spliced into a longer word (`eval "git $(echo push) origin HEAD:main"`), is now denied. Output that lands as data, such as `eval "echo $(echo git push …)"`, is not. Substitutions whose output is only known at run time (`eval "$(ssh-agent -s)"`) are unchanged.
