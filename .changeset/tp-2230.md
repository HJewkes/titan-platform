"@titan-design/tool-guard": patch
---

Read the literal output of a substitution that `eval` or a shell's `-c` runs. A push or merge reached through `eval "$(cat <<'E' … E)"`, a cat here-string, `echo`, `printf` or a backquote, including the arithmetic form `eval "$(cat <<'E' $((X)) E)"`, is now denied. Substitutions whose output is only known at run time (`eval "$(ssh-agent -s)"`) are unchanged.
