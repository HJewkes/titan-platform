Shepherd no longer asks the owner to merge a fix-first-runaway or no-progress head (D8, `GATE_DEMOTIONS.fixFirstRunaway`, on by default). One fresh reviewer gets the run's FIX_FIRST trail as a convergence review: its MERGE takes the normal land path, and its FIX_FIRST sends the repo's seat (or the hub seat) a `fix-first-planner` notice while the run waits for a new head. A moved head re-arms the review. The reviewer brief gains `convergence`, which asks for a `Convergence:` section tagging each open finding Important, Nit or Pre-existing.
