Every error-severity L0 rule the script stage can fail now has a repair hint of its own, and a coverage test fails on any rule that lacks one. The Q-W1 hint names the early VERDICT or STAKE beats and says to put "you" (or the stake's who) and the stake's number in one sentence of the first. The new hints cover Q-W1 to Q-W10, Q-F2, Q-F7 to Q-F13, Q-V1 to Q-V9, Q-E1 to Q-E4, Q-E7, Q-E9, Q-E11, Q-E15, Q-E16 and Q-E18. Each one names the failing beats, chapters or sentences, and the edit to make.
