The `chart` page kind draws the `baseline`, `axisBreak`, `scale` and `legend` slots. Bar and line axes start at the baseline and name it when it is not zero, a broken axis draws a break mark at the origin, a colour scale colours each point by its value (a diverging scale centred on the baseline), and a legend keys each series to its colour. The render stage version moves to m10c5.
