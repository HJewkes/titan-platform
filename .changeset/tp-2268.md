`service deploy` watches a restarted build for a short window (`--watch-window`, `service.deployWatchMs`, default 2 min): serve must keep answering `/health` on the same sha and pid with no new failed runs. A failed restart, sha check or watch now rolls back, verifies the old sha is serving, pauses every deploy with `deploy-pause.json` (a newer sha does not bypass it) and files one incident notice to the hub seat and the owner queue. A rollback that does not verify raises one `ALERT:` and is never retried. `service pause` shows the pause and `service pause --clear` lifts it under the deploy lock.
