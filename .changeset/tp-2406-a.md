Split the runner into `execSide`, `judge` and a report transport (`encodeReport`, `encodeError`, `decodeReport`). Each exec side writes capped, gzip and base64 encoded reports with repo-relative names; `judge` recomputes the plan from git, decodes the reports under an 8 MB inflate cap and prints the one fix-proof/v1 line, spawning nothing but git. The base side now installs and builds before any head file is overlaid, and always runs its tests twice. `run` composes the two sides and the judge in-process.
