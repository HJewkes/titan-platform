Template beats fit their text to the stage box: every beat-scale token shrinks with `--beat-fit` down to the 40 px type floor, and a stat card holds its settled figure's width. Content still past the box at the floor is a row naming the beat, the element, the px and a shorten hint, and render's pre-capture stage-fit scan reports every such row in one `StageOverflow` instead of stopping at the first page error. Render stage version `m10c8` to `m10c9`.
