Hold a ci-red wake that only repeats a frozen main's failures. With a freeze open on the PR's repo, a red head whose failing checks all fail on the freeze's red sha spends no repair and wakes nobody: `sh-freeze-hold` records why, and `sh-freeze-wait` waits for the thaw or a new head before the next round re-reads CI. A failing check that main does not share, no freeze, or the fixer's own PR wakes as before.
