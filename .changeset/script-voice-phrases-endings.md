Three script-voice changes from the ai-voice research. Q-V11 gains two warning rows. `register:phrases` counts the phrases in `doctrine/voice-phrases.json`, a corpus-derived list of phrases our scripts say far more often than human explainers, against a human-creator band. `register:shortest-last` reports the share of beats that end on their shortest sentence against chance, and never warns. The rewrite plan names a directed beat's stock phrases while the phrases row fails. The rewrite guard refuses `closer-added`, a new short last line the original did not end on, and counts "a number of", "various" and "hundreds" as vague stand-ins for a moved number. The script stage's planner guide gets a specificity line (`register-AV3`). The L1 script rubric caps J6 for vague beats and J7 for moral or summing-up closers, so the L1 eval_version changes. Stage versions: script x12, rewrite x7, eval x7.
