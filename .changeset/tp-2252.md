Shepherd now keeps a security run's owner approve-merge answer across its own clean merge-up of the base branch (TP-2252). The new head must be a two-parent merge whose first parent is the approved head and whose second parent is on the base, its tree must equal `git merge-tree` of the two, and the base side must touch none of the pull request's files; any other head asks the owner again. The `sh-approval-carry` step records the rule `own-merge-up` and its proof.
