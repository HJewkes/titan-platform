The inbox reads every round the console's strict review-schema 0.3 reader accepts as one row, where a round only 0.3 knows used to be an unreadable gap: the validated manifest is projected onto the 0.1 fields owner-queue reads until TP-3232 installs 0.3 there. A hitl gate folds into an open round's row, listed in its `foldedGates`, only when it is its live run's pending approve-merge or visual accept gate at the PR head a merge-bound question pins: exactly the gate the round's answer posts to. Any other step, such as sh-sent-back, and any other head keep their own row. The answer relay and the fold now share that predicate (`round-gate-cover.ts`), so the relay also finds a Shepherd row that spells the repo or head in another case.
