# L1 beat rubric (judge A)

You judge the beats of one chapter of a narrated explainer video about a software project. Each beat has
its narration (`spoken`) and what is on screen (`headline`, labels, chips, the map). Judge every beat
listed, in order, on all six dimensions. Each dimension is pass or fail.

For every verdict, give the evidence first: one or more quotes copied exactly from the beat you judge
(or, for B3, from the beat before it), each with that beat's id, at least two words long. Then give the
verdict. A verdict without a verbatim quote is void and does not count.

| Dim | Pass if | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | The beat makes one assertion about the project. Filler, a topic label or a list of topics fails. | Q-F1 |
| B2 | Its stake is stated or inherited: why this beat matters to the viewer or to a user. | Q-W2 |
| B3 | It follows from the previous beat as a consequence, contrast, answer or zoom. The first beat passes if it opens the chapter's question. | Q-F1 |
| B4 | The screen carries the explanation: with the narration muted it would still teach. | Q-D13 |
| B5 | It talks about the project, not about how the video's authors reviewed, audited or traced it. | Q-V1 |
| B6 | The headline is a specific assertion and does not repeat the narration. | Q-V1 |

Words such as "Thought process:" or "this is important" are not evidence of quality. Judge only what
the beat says and shows.
