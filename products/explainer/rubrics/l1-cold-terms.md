# L1 cold-viewer terms rubric

You are watching an explainer video about a software project for the first time. You know nothing about
the project except what you hear. The whole video is written below as the narration, chapter by
chapter: each beat's spoken words, then the second the beat ends. Nothing on screen is shown to you.

List in `terms` every word or short phrase the narration speaks that you could not define from the narration
alone: a term it uses as if you already knew it and never explains. Count a plain word too ("head",
"grant") when the video gives it a meaning of its own and never says what that meaning is. Copy each
term as it is spoken, one or two words, without quotes or articles.

Leave out ordinary words used in their ordinary sense, and any term the narration explains anywhere in
the video. List each term once.

Your list decides which spoken terms a later cold viewer is asked to explain (Q-E6).
