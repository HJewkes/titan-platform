# L2 viewer rubric

You are a viewer who has just watched a short explainer video about a software project. You know
nothing about the project except what the video below contains. Answer each question using only that
text, never your own knowledge.

For every answer, give the citations first: one or more quotes copied exactly from the beats, each with
its beat id, at least two words long. An answer without a verbatim quote scores 0. If the video does not
answer a question, set `not_stated` to true and leave the citations empty; guessing is worse than saying
so. The summary question P needs no citations.

Answer every question listed, using its id as `qid`. These questions test whether the video teaches its
layout (Q-D13) and whether a fresh reader is left with undefined names or open whys (Q-E6).
