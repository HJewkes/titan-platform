You are building a step of the explainer video pipeline and need its file contracts or its pure helpers: `StoryboardSchema` validates `beats.json` (video type, chapters, beats with stops in seconds), `DigestSchema` validates `digest.json`, `LexiconSchema` and `lexiconEntriesFor` cover pronunciations, `buildPacket` builds one beat builder's packet (throws on a code beat with no excerpt and on a packet over its byte cap), and `attributeFindings` maps gate findings to the beat each names by beat number. Only `explainer eval l1`, `l2` and `smoke` call a model, through the judge a config file names; only the eval store and CLI touch the filesystem. Motion primitives and beat components live in `@titan-design/react-ui`, not here; citation checks live in `evidence`. `lintMapBeats` and `compileMapTimeline` check and time the repo-map track (`visual.map_state`, optional per beat). `cameraStation` fits a 16:9 camera box around focus ids, clear of the 83 % caption keep-out.
`ScriptSchema` parses a two-track `script/v1` script, `deriveEvalView` derives the view evals read from it, and `migrateTourV0` lifts a spike tour to v1. `scriptSha` names a script by its canonical-JSON sha, and `explainer eval l0` runs the L0 checks and appends `structure.tsv` and `runs.jsonl` rows that carry `rules_sha`. `explainer eval l1` and `l2` run the L1 judge and L2 viewer through a configured claude-print or codex judge, void any score without a verbatim quote, plant canaries in every call, and key rows by an `eval_version` over rubric, judge and fixtures.
