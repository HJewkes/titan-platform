# explainer: use this when

<!-- One to three sentences for a reader deciding whether to reuse this or build something new. Feeds CAPABILITIES.md. -->

You are building a step of the explainer video pipeline and need its file contracts or its pure helpers: `StoryboardSchema` validates `beats.json` (video type, chapters, beats with stops in seconds), `DigestSchema` validates `digest.json`, `LexiconSchema` and `lexiconEntriesFor` cover pronunciations, `buildPacket` builds one beat builder's packet (throws on a code beat with no excerpt and on a packet over its byte cap), and `attributeFindings` maps gate findings to the beat each names by beat number. Only `explainer eval l1`, `l2`, `cold` and `smoke` call a model, through the judge a config file names; only the eval store, the CLI, the owner kit, `loadDoctrine` and `gatherReview` touch the filesystem. Motion primitives and beat components live in `@titan-design/react-ui`, not here; citation checks live in `evidence`. `lintMapBeats`, `lintMapCues` and `compileMapTimeline` check and time the repo-map track (`visual.map_state`, optional per beat), and `toDiagramMapTimeline` turns it into titan-design's `MapTimeline` keyframes. `cameraStation` fits a 16:9 camera box around focus ids, clear of the 83 % caption keep-out.
`ScriptSchema` parses a two-track `script/v1` script, `deriveEvalView` derives the view evals read from it, and `migrateTourV0` lifts a spike tour to v1. `scriptSha` names a script by its canonical-JSON sha, and `explainer eval l0` runs the L0 checks and appends `structure.tsv` and `runs.jsonl` rows that carry `rules_sha`. For an owner's blind read, `prepareOwnerSession` writes two-track storyboards under a sealed key and `recordOwnerSession` appends `owner.tsv` rows before it opens the key. `loadRuleRegistry` reads the shipped Q-rule registry (`doctrine/rules.yaml`), and `unimplementedL0Rules` reports rules that claim a check no registered L0 check emits. `loadDoctrine` reads the craft doctrine registry (`doctrine/craft/*.yaml` with its vocabulary and evidence ledger), and `checkDoctrine` reports missing evidence ids, grades the cited rows do not state, live code-checked rules left in a prompt enforcement, unresolved `qrule` refs and scope errors. `sliceForStage` returns the craft rules one pipeline stage loads for a video type, audience and beats (prompt, base and packet prose, checks, token names, profile params), never a code-enforced rule in prose, and `findPromptLeaks` flags a prompt that names a code-enforced doctrine rule or an implemented-L0 Q-rule by id, alias or text. `explainer eval l1` and `l2` run the L1 judge and L2 viewer through a configured claude-print or codex judge, void any score without a verbatim quote, plant canaries in every call, and key rows by an `eval_version` over rubric, judge and fixtures. For pace evals, `fromScriptV1` and `fromSpikeScript` project a script to `PaceBeat`s (the items each beat puts on screen, their role from `CUE_KIND_ROLES`, and which are new), `readSpikeScript` reads a Python-emitted spike script.json loosely and lists the fields it ignores, and `readTiming` reads heard durations from a words.json. `runCold` streams that track to a cold viewer one chapter per call, carrying only its three-line summary, and reports undefined items per chapter minute from generated questions scored against verbatim quotes; `explainer eval cold` runs it three times by default and appends its rows and run records under an `eval_version` that includes the judge. `paceCheck` (in `L0_CHECKS`) and `paceRows` lint that item track: Q-E7 at most 4 new items a beat (error), and as warnings Q-E8 seconds a new item, Q-E9 15 s to digest between dense beats and Q-E10 new items a chapter minute (`chapterPace`). It also lints seating and reuse: Q-E11 a beat that states an invariant needs a map and states at most one (error), Q-E13 `reuseFindings` names reused `origin: v2` beats that fail Q-E7, Q-E8, Q-E9 or Q-E11 with their heard seconds to re-voice, and Q-E12 (`seatRoutes`) and Q-E14 (`explainRoutes`) only route beats to the L1 judges and never gate. `explainer eval pace` runs only those lints on a spike or script/v1 file, with heard durations from `--timing`, appends their rows and prints items a minute per chapter, then the reused beats to re-voice when there are any.
To calibrate checks against labels, `LabelsSchema` parses a `labels/v1` labelled set (the v4 one is `fixtures/spike/redesign-explainer-v4/labels.json`) and `calibrate` scores structure rows against it: precision and recall per check, with borderline labels counting as neither, and recall on the owner-named beats over error-severity rows; `explainer eval calibrate` prints that report.
For a repo explainer, `gatherReview` reads a finished repo review (pinned clone, final label, `unread=0`) into a `review-bundle/v1` and schema-checked `ExplainerFact`s, tour kinds included, and refuses with coded diagnostics (`GATHER_CODES`) rather than throwing.
