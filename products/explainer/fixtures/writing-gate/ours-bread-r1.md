The kettle sits on the stove all morning. Steam rises from the spout in thin ribbons. Nobody in the kitchen pays it any mind. The cook is busy with the flour.

Flour goes into the big wooden bowl. Water follows it in a slow stream. The dough comes together under two firm hands. It feels sticky at first, then smooth.

The dough rests under a clean cloth. Time does most of the work here. Small bubbles form deep inside the mass. The whole bowl begins to swell.

The oven needs to be very hot. The cook slides the tray onto the rack. The door closes with a heavy thud. Now comes the hard part, the waiting.

Heat sets the crust in minutes. The smell fills the whole house. A golden shell forms across the top. The loaf is ready when it sounds hollow.

The cook lifts the bread out carefully. It cools on a wire rack by the window. Everyone gathers around the table. The first slice is always the best one.
